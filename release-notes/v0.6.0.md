## Rota — a second tab

Monospace Timetable now does the other recurring job every school has: checking
things on a rolling turn. IT rooms cleaned two a week, fire extinguishers four
a month, a minibus walked round every Friday.

It works the way the timetable does. You give it a list, say how often and how
many at a time, and it produces the whole year — then exports it as a formatted
Excel workbook.

**Pick a starting point.** Eight presets ship, each with its own columns:
IT room check, fire extinguisher, fire door, minibus walk-round, PAT testing,
legionella flush, fridge temperature, and a blank one. A preset is a starting
point, not a mode — change any of it afterwards and nothing puts it back.

**Half turns.** Mark a small room as taking half a turn and the rota pairs it
with another one automatically. The line printed under the title — `N11 + A4,
H22 + T11, T21 + T22` — is worked out from the rota underneath it rather than
typed, so the two cannot disagree.

**It follows your school year.** Week letters and holidays come from the same
calendar the timetable prints, so `Week A` and `Half Term` mean the same thing
on both sheets. A school with no timetable in the file can give a rota its own
start and end dates instead.

**Two exports.** *With data* prints what has been recorded. *Blank template*
prints the tick columns empty whatever is recorded — the copy that goes on the
clipboard.

Your rotas save into the same `.json` as the timetable, so one file is still
the school's year.
